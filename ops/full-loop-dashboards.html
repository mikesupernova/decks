0. Rules that apply to every dashboard

  1. Dual scoring (D-007). Every KPI shows two scores on the same denominator: controllable (misses the 3PL caused: P-01..P-06, plus D-01 with owner 3PL) and total (all misses). Carrier, supplier, port and customer-driven misses count in the total and carry an outside 3PL control tag. U-00 (unconfirmed) stays out of the controllable count and is shown as its own flag. Controllable is never shown without total. No add-backs.
  2. Exceptions first. The order and container views open on the exception list (what broke or will break, owner, how long open). Rows that are on track appear only as counts. Each screen is calm and green by default, so red stands out.
  3. Early warning, not a lagging score. An order goes at risk when its projected ETA passes the promised date, not when it is already late. A container is flagged when it sits at or is heading to a port with an active risk row in freight/watchtower-log.md.
  4. Customer-service signals are background only. Ticket volume and contact reasons can raise an early-warning note (for example "won't power on" contacts rising for one SKU). They never feed a KPI, score, tier or credit.
  5. Money belongs to Contracts & Finance. Spend, revenue, return value and reroute cost come from C&F. Other bots quote C&F and never compute money.
  6. Data source = confirm on Day 1 (board M-02: HR system, BI tool, data warehouse access). Candidate systems are named below; none is confirmed. The real schema stays unknown until M-01 closes.

Metric Definition Score

Orders at risk Open orders where projected ETA > promised date total + controllable (cause = 3PL)
Orders on watch Open orders where projected ETA is within the buffer of the promised date (ETA ≤ promised but buffer < 1 business day) count only
Requested-date slip Projected ETA − customer requested date; separate metric, does not set at risk (D-008) per order
Slip (days) Projected ETA − promised date, in business days per order

Projected ETA Max of (planned release + site release-to-ship median, stock-available date for short lines, inbound container ETA + dock-to-stock for lines waiting on inbound) + lane transit median (freight/carrier-lane-scorecard.md) per order
Cause tag 3PL (P-01..P-06) · carrier (C-01/C-02) · supplier (S-..) · port/event · customer · unconfirmed (U-00) per order

Columns on the list: order, customer, rep, ship site, promised, projected ETA, slip, cause tag (inside/outside 3PL control), owner, next action, hours open.

  • Headline goes amber when customers on track < 97%; red when < 95% (total score).

3. Container visibility (Appendix K2; wall screen 3)

Purpose: every inbound container with its port, ETA and status, a port-risk flag, and a suggested reprioritize or reroute move. Built around exceptions.

Headline (EXAMPLE): 17 of 26 containers clear · 9 flagged · 6 at or heading to LA/LB. On plan: 24 of 26 total · 25 of 26 controllable (only EX-CNTR-033 is 3PL-caused).

Metric Definition Score
Containers clear Active inbound containers with no flag (port risk, watch, late, dwell, LFD) ÷ all active inbound count
Containers on plan Projected arrival at the 3PL ≤ planned date ÷ active inbound containers total + controllable
Port-risk exposure Containers at or due within 7 days at a port with an active watchtower risk row (strike, weather, congestion, closure) count + list
Dwell at port Days since discharge / availability without pickup per container
Last free day (LFD) gap LFD − today (demurrage risk) per container
Linked orders Customer orders whose stock is on the container (from §1) count
Status Booked · Rolled · On water · Discharged · Available · Picked up · At 3PL · Received per container

Controllable for containers = misses the 3PL caused after the container reaches its door (dock appointment refused or pushed, dock-to-stock over SLA). Port, carrier, customs and supplier delays count in the total, tagged outside 3PL control.

Suggested moves (rules; each move is ranked and tracked on K6, §10): pull available boxes before a risk deadline; ask the carrier to discharge at an unaffected terminal; divert on-water boxes to another port (for example Oakland or Seattle/Tacoma for EX-USW) when the cost is approved; air a class-A top-up on the air lane (EX-L02); fill linked customer orders from another site. Reroute costs come from C&F before any move.

Alert thresholds (ADOPTED, D-012; review after month 1 of real data):

  • Port-risk flag: any active watchtower risk row for the port, with the event deadline shown (EXAMPLE now: LA/LB ILWU Local 26 watchmen, deadline 2026-11-04 17:00 PT, real public event, outcome pending).
  • Dwell at port > 3 days, or LFD within 2 days → alert Freight & Trade.
  • ETA slip > 3 days vs plan, or status "Rolled" → alert Freight & Trade and re-run §1 projections for linked orders.
  • Any container with linked top-10 or big-order stock in a flagged port → escalate to Mike.
  • Reroute spend (D-010, method approved): Freight & Trade may approve one container's move if cost ≤ min(CAP, 50% × avoided exposure), cost < avoided exposure, and month-to-date + cost ≤ monthly cap. Single container only, no splitting, logged within 24 h, never sign, pay or commit terms; limit = 0 without a tariff or quote for that box. Above the limit → Mike. CAP and monthly cap are set on Day 1 against the company approval policy (Mike's own limit is the ceiling); until then every reroute cost goes to Mike. Method: finance/d010-reroute-spend-limit-proposal.md.

Owner bot: Freight & Trade (status, port risk, moves). Contracts & Finance (reroute, demurrage and detention cost). 3PL Ops Lead (dock appointment and dock-to-stock). Data to confirm Day 1: forwarder / carrier visibility portal or TMS (container status and ETA), ASN feed (freight/asn-sscc-requirements.md), data warehouse if loaded; port risk from freight/watchtower-log.md (public).

4. Returns (Appendix K3; wall screen 5)

Purpose: see products returned at a high rate (rate and value), customers who return a lot, why things come back, and flag what needs action.

Headline (EXAMPLE, trailing 4 weeks): return rate 2.0% of units shipped (total) · 0.40% controllable (3PL-caused) · return value 2.6% of shipped value (C&F) · 2 SKUs need action · 1 customer to review.

Metric Definition Score
Return rate (units) Units returned (RMA received) ÷ units shipped, same SKU, trailing 4 weeks total + controllable
Return value % Value of returned units ÷ value shipped (C&F) % only, no dollars
SKU return rate vs network SKU rate ÷ network rate ratio
Customer return rate Units a customer returned ÷ units it bought, trailing 12 weeks per customer
Return reason mix Share of returned units by reason code %
Return cycle time RMA issued → credit or replacement shipped median, P90

Reason codes → control tag: wrong item shipped, damaged at 3PL / packing = 3PL (controllable); damaged in transit = carrier (outside); DOA / defect = supplier (outside); ordered wrong / no longer needed = customer (outside); unknown = unconfirmed.

Alert thresholds (ADOPTED, D-012; review after month 1 of real data):

  • SKU ACTION: rate ≥ 2× the network rate or ≥ 5%, with ≥ 30 units shipped in the window. WATCH: ≥ 1.5× network.
  • Any 3PL-coded reason (wrong item, packing damage) ≥ 1% of a SKU's units → CAPA review with the site (the CAPA trigger is 3PL-tagged misses only, decisions.md 10-26).
  • Customer REVIEW: ≥ 10% of units returned (≥ 50 units bought) or top 5 by return value → rep review.
  • Return value % > 3% of shipped value in a month → C&F note to Mike.
  • Customer-service contacts on a return theme: background early-warning note only.

Owner bot: 3PL Ops Lead (rates, reasons, reverse-logistics flow on lanes EX-L17, EX-L18). Contracts & Finance (value, credits, recovery). Freight & Trade (carrier damage claims). Data to confirm Day 1: RMA / returns system (may sit in the ERP or the CS platform), 3PL returns receipts (WMS), data warehouse for joins, BI tool view.

Spend moves: avoided exposure 157 vs est. cost 90 (cost index points, EXAMPLE); the 4 moves that pass the 50% test cost 26. Full EXAMPLE list: deck Appendix K6.
