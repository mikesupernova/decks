Discussion · for Matt · first 30 days

Questions for Matt

Twelve questions for the first 30 days. The answers replace the assumptions.

01Network
Which 3PLs and sites do we use today, in which countries, and what does each handle (B2B, partner/distributor, RMA, kitting, spares)?
02Contracts
Where are the contracts, rate cards, SLAs, penalty/credit clauses, and renewal dates?
03KPIs
What KPIs do we measure today, with what definitions, from which system, and who trusts them?
04Volume
What are monthly volumes (orders, lines, units, pallets) by site, and our peak pattern (quarter-end)?
05Cost
What is total 3PL spend and cost per unit/order by site, and what is the budget for this year?
06Customers
What are the top 3 customer/partner complaints tied to fulfillment?
07Flows
How do serialization, firmware/config, and returns (RMA) flow through each 3PL?
08Systems
What WMS/ERP/TMS integrations exist (EDI/API), and where are manual handoffs?
09Trade
Who owns trade compliance, Incoterms, duties/tariffs, and importer of record per region?
10Decision rights
What decisions am I empowered to make alone, and what needs approval (spend, new sites, contract changes)?
11Success
What does success look like to you at 6 and 12 months?
12Risk
Which 3PL relationships are at risk, and are any RFPs or network changes already planned?

Proposed next step: answer what we can before Nov 16; the rest feed week 1, when the real site list replaces the three-site assumption.
